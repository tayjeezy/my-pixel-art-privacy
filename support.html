Frequently asked questions

How do I make a pixel artwork?

Tap New Artwork, take a photo or choose one from your library, and it's turned into a colour-by-number grid you can fill in.

How does colouring work?

Pick a colour from the palette, then tap or drag across the matching squares. Use Hint if you get stuck, and Reveal to peek at the original photo.

Can I save or share my art?

Yes — once you're done, save it to your Photos or share it straight from the app.

Are my photos private?

Absolutely. All processing happens on your device and your photos are never uploaded to any server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is the app really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.
