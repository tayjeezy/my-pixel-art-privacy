Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how My Pixel Pics ("the app") handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

The app requests access to your device's camera and/or photo library so you can select or take a photo to convert into a coloring page. This is the only personal information the app interacts with.

How your photos are used

All photo processing happens entirely on your device. When you select or take a photo, it is converted into a coloring page locally using on-device image processing. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server, and are not accessible to us or to any third party.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your photos or any other personal information with third parties. We do not have access to your photos at any point.

Analytics and third-party services

The app uses a privacy-focused analytics provider that does not collect personal data, to understand how the app's features are used (for example, how many times the app is opened, or whether a coloring page was created from the camera or the photo library).

This provider does not collect your photos, your name, your email address, or any other information that identifies you. Each signal is associated with an anonymous identifier that is hashed twice and rotates regularly, so it cannot be traced back to you or linked to your activity across sessions. This data is not used for tracking, is not linked to your identity, and is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

We do not use any advertising or crash-reporting services. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and the revision date above will reflect the change.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all photo processing occurs on-device and nothing is transmitted or stored remotely, no data about a child using the app (or a photo of a child) ever leaves the device.

Camera and photo library permissions

You can grant or revoke camera and photo library access at any time via your device's Settings app. Revoking access will prevent the app from converting new photos but will not affect any coloring pages already created.

Data security

Because processing happens entirely on your device, your photos are protected by your device's own security (such as passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID) rather than by any server-side security measures on our part, since no such server exists for this data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
